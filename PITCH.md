# Pitch kit: BMW 3D Showroom

## Who to contact
The dealer's **marketing manager** or **general sales manager**, not the website team. BMW runs the dealer's website, but the dealer controls its own showroom screens, events and social media.

## Outreach message (email or LinkedIn)

Subject: A 3D showroom for [Dealer name], ready to try

Hi [Name],

I build interactive 3D car showrooms. I made a working demo with the M4 Competition and the X5: customers can open the bonnet, lift out the engine parts, look at the brakes piece by piece, sit inside and even "drive" the car with engine sound.

It is designed for the things BMW's online configurator doesn't do:
- **A touchscreen in the showroom.** It runs on its own, plays an attract loop when idle, and turns interest into a sales-advisor request.
- **Launch events and social media.** It records striking videos of any model taken apart.
- **Lead capture.** Every "book a test drive" or "talk to an advisor" request goes straight to your inbox.

Here it is: [link]. Try it on a laptop or tablet.

I can set it up with your name and your lineup. Would you have 15 minutes this week for me to show you in person?

Best,
Omar Hossam
[phone]

## What they get (one-page summary)
- Their lineup in interactive 3D, starting with the models they want to push.
- Showroom kiosk mode: fullscreen, attract loop, automatic reset and on-the-spot lead capture.
- Web version for their social media and campaigns, working on phones.
- Leads emailed to the sales team.
- Their branding: name, contact details and colours.
- Updates when new models arrive.

## Pricing (decide before the meeting)
Suggested structure, which Omar should adjust for his market:
- **Set-up:** a fee per car (roughly a day of work each, more if a 3D model has to be bought).
- **Kiosk package:** installation on their touchscreen PC plus the attract loop.
- **Monthly care:** hosting, fixes and adding new models.

## Be upfront about
- **Brand approval:** official dealers need BMW sign-off before using BMW names and imagery in their own materials. Offer to adjust the site to whatever BMW asks for.
- **Models per car:** each car needs a 3D model. Free CC-BY models exist for some cars (BMW published the X5 itself), and others need buying (about $20–150 each).
- **Indicative figures:** confirm the numbers against the dealer's own price and spec sheets.
- **Engine sound:** it is synthesised, not a recording.

## Demo checklist before a meeting
1. Put their name and phone number in `dealer.js`.
2. Open `?kiosk` on a tablet or laptop. Wait 60 s to show the attract loop, then touch to explore.
3. Show, in order: the M4 bonnet and engine parts, the brakes side by side, step inside, drive, the X5 tailgate, and the configurator.
4. Submit a test request and show the email arriving.
